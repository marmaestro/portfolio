**Company/Institution | Date Range**

2-3 sentence summary: What you built, the core challenge you faced, and what you actually spent most of your time doing. Be specific and direct.

### Overview

<em>**Role/Position:**</em> Your role  
<em>**Context:**</em> Team size/Duration/Type of project  
<em>**Your Responsibilities:**</em> What you were actually responsible for  
<em>**Tools:**</em> Technologies, engines, languages, tools

1-2 paragraphs: Set up the context. What was the project? What were you originally brought on to do? How did that evolve?

### The Core Problem/Concept

2-3 paragraphs: What was the main technical/creative/design challenge? Why was it hard? What made this interesting or different? Use "I" and be direct about what you discovered or realized.

### Technical Work / What I Actually Did

3-4 paragraphs covering your main contributions. Each paragraph should focus on one system/feature/area. Format:

#### System/Feature Name
What you built and why. Include technical details but explain them clearly. What decisions did you make? What tradeoffs? What shortcuts did you take and later fix?

Keep it concrete. "Built X using Y to solve Z" rather than "Responsible for X."

### The Making Of / Challenges

2-3 paragraphs: The human part. What pushed you outside your comfort zone? What took longer than expected? What would you do differently? Be honest about mistakes and what you learned from them. This is where your personality shows through.

### Outcomes / What I Learned

1-2 paragraphs: What did you actually deliver? What did this teach you that you didn't know before? Focus on skills/insights that transferred beyond this specific project.

<em>**Note:**</em> Any additional context, NDA notes, links to demos, etc.

Use for project cards/listings:

#### Option 1 (Technical focus)
Project type at Company/Institution. Main technical achievement in one sentence. Key challenge or learning in one sentence.

#### Option 2 (Personal narrative)
Time period doing main work. What made it difficult/interesting. Key technical work.

#### Option 3 (Concept-driven)
What you built to solve problem. Technical approach. Key insight or outcome.

#### Option 4 (Direct)
Role on project type. Main systems built. Technologies used.
