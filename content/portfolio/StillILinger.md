An experimental _artgame_ about feeling lost, depression, and life. Combining real-life film photography with digital media, this story shows a fictional city and the new challenges of moving somewhere unknown means.

This game is to be released as my Bachelor's Thesis, and will be further developed upon graduation.


### WARNING!

The current version of the game was built with an editor version with a severe security vulnerability. You can read more about it [here](https://unity.com/security/sept-2025-01). It is not available for download any more, but will be rebuilt and redeployed soon. Thanks for your patience.
