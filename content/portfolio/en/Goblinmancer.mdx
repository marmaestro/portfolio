## BackToBits Studio | In development

Tower defence auto-battler in development.
A game I'm working on as part of [BackToBits Studio](https://backtobitsstudio.com).

# Role

I work as <mark>lead programmer</mark> of the coding team.
We're rebuilding the original prototype from the ground up in ECS architecture using Unity.
My roles in this position include reviewing the design choices my team makes, as well as implementing some of them.

I also work as <mark>project manager</mark> and <mark>producer</mark>.
This means coordinating the team, making sure everything gets done in time and, most importantly, properly.
I work closely with the design and narrative team, and will do so with art teams once they leave their pre-production phase.

# About the Game

This is a fast-paced game in which the player must defend the Goblinmancer's magic tower from incoming enemy goblins.
Their resurrected bodies become <mark>Zombies</mark> which, together with their <mark>Blood</mark>, are the resources of the game.
The necromancer goblin then spends these resources into different types of defence against enemies: buildings, units and spells.

The prototype has been taken to fairs, in which it received acclaim for its silly, fast-paced nature, as well as its core fundamentals.
