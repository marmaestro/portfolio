**Independent | Releasing**

DevSide is a Spanish-speaking podcast about video games, and many more things.
Each episode has its own theme, focused from the point of view of videogame development.
We want to share the *developer side*, the tape's *dev* side.

<mark>**Roles:**</mark> Direction, hosting, editing, writing

Season 1 aka "Pilot" is recorded with the co-production of UJI's [LabCom](https://www.uji.es/institucional/estructura/entitats/labcom/?urlRedirect=https://www.uji.es/institucional/estructura/entitats/labcom/&url=/institucional/estructura/entitats/labcom/&urlRedirect=https://www.uji.es/institucional/estructura/entitats/labcom/&url=/institucional/estructura/entitats/labcom/).
