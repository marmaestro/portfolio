**Punic Goose** presents a short-form documentary on slow gaming and gaming slow. We crack jokes while adhering to the seriousness divulgation needs. It features an interview with [Alberto Porta-Pérez](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alberto-porta-p%C3%A9rez-0502b1159/), Game Studies PhD in ludonarratives, ludomusicology, childhood and moral dilemmas.

Made in collaboration with [LabCom UJI](https://www.uji.es/institucional/estructura/entitats/labcom/).

Video produced for _Theory and Practice of Audiovisual Production_, part of the _Bachelor's Degree in Video Game Design and Development_ at Universitat Jaume I.
