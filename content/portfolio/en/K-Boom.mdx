# Every bomb you place is a step closer to your goal.

Camilo the chameleon has an arsenal of bombs at his disposal. Choose your strategy before planting bombs in the right order, and then push the trigger!

As the bombs explode in order, things might fall right into place or... not at all.
Try and test, plan and strategise, forge ahead!

*K-Boom!* follows a world-level structure that introduces new elements in each world. Each element can be moved, toppled or blown up in its own particular way.
A great deal of level design has gone into these!
