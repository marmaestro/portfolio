## Independent | January 2026 - Present

A Spanish-speaking podcast on indie video game development.
We're currently pre-producing Season 2.

# Role

I <mark>co-produce</mark> DEVSIDE with my co-hosts, and direct most (if not all) programs.
I'm also in charge of all <mark>audio edition</mark> and <mark>graphic design</mark>.

# About the Podcast

DEVSIDE is a Spanish-speaking podcast on the development of video games.
We focus on indie, since it's the part of the industry we're most in contact with.

Each program has its own theme, always related to the world of video games.
Some programs feature guests, too!

Season 1 was produced with the support of UJI's [LabCom](https://www.uji.es/institucional/estructura/entitats/labcom/).
