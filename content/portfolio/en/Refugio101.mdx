## Vox UJI Ràdio | March 2023 - June 2025

A Spanish-speaking university radio programme on video games and news.

# Role

I acted as <mark>director</mark> of the programme, as well as <mark>producer</mark>.
Each member handled the writing of their own sections, essays and reviews.

# About the Podcast

Every few years, this radio programme changes hands from seniors to freshmen.
I was handed the programme to recruit the 4th generation and direct it.

Refugio 101 is a weekly radio programme hosted by students of UJI's Bachelor's Degree in Video Game Design and Development.
Each 1-hour episode reviews the current state of the industry, and then features varying sections.
The programs I directed consisted mostly of essays and a special review section called "Heaven or Hell".

We passed it over to the next holders, and now work on our new podcast: [DEVSIDE](/portfolio/devside).
