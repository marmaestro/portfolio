**Vox UJI Ràdio | March 2023 - June 2025**

<mark>Refugio 101</mark> is a Spanish-speaking video games radio programme published at UJI's IP radio station. For an hour, you can listen to news, essays and reviews every week.

### Overview

<mark>**Roles:**</mark> Direction, hosting, editing, writing

Every few years, this radio programme changes hands from seniors to freshmen. For the 4th generation, it was me—along with my colleagues—that got to work on it.

We are currently preparing a new project called [DevSide](/portfolio/en/devside). Premiers in April.
