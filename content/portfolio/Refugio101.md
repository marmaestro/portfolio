_Refugio 101_ is a Spanish-speaking video games radio programme published at UJI's IP radio station. You can listen to news, essays and reviews every week.

Every few years, the baton that is hosting this radio programme changes hands. For the 4th edition, it was me —along with my colleagues— that got to work on it. This 4th generation of _Refugio 101_ is currently preparing a new podcast.

It is currently in the hands of the 5th generation. I couldn't be happier about this project staying alive and being better every week that passes.

Most of my relevant works for this are hosted on my [Medium](https://medium.com/@marmaestro/list/refugio-101-50bad156a1a7) page and will be soon posted here.
