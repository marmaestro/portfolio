This includes managing the [studio website](https://backtobitsstudio.com), as well as <mark>managing projects</mark>.
I work together with my teammates [Raúl](https://www.linkedin.com/in/raplajo/)
    and [Álvaro](https://www.linkedin.com/in/%C3%A1lvaro-chuan-9003a4254/) as administrator.
